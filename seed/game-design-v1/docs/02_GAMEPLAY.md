# Gameplay

## Core loop

1. Observe the settlement and upcoming growth.
2. Inspect demand and forecast.
3. Choose infrastructure.
4. Place or reroute it.
5. Preview the predicted result.
6. Run the day.
7. Watch the town operate.
8. If a problem appears, pause at the exact failure.
9. Diagnose the cause.
10. Modify the network.
11. Complete the stage.
12. Unlock the next growth stage.

The game alternates between thoughtful planning and watching the living miniature world run.

## Planning mode

Time is paused.

The player can:
- place infrastructure,
- remove it,
- move it,
- upgrade it,
- reroute lines,
- inspect forecasts,
- scrub through time,
- compare placements,
- preview bottlenecks.

There is no planning timer.

## Simulation mode

The player presses **Run Day**.

Recommended speed:
- 1 day ≈ 2 real minutes at 1×
- 1 day ≈ 30 seconds at 4×

Controls:
- Pause
- 1×
- 4×
- Analyse

Analyse runs the full period instantly and jumps to the first failure.

## Example run

06:00 — household demand begins rising  
08:00 — café and workshop open  
11:00 — solar generation becomes strong  
13:00 — battery charges  
17:30 — household demand climbs  
18:30 — solar falls  
19:15 — transformer reaches 96%  
20:00 — station load starts  
20:10 — transformer overload

The simulation pauses automatically.

The game explains:

**Village Transformer overloaded**  
Demand: 1.46 MW  
Capacity: 1.20 MW

The player can scrub backward and inspect what caused the problem.

## Growth

Growth happens between successful stages, not randomly while the simulation runs.

Future construction should appear as:
- marked plots,
- construction sites,
- ghosted future buildings.

This lets the player anticipate future load without knowing every exact number.

The same electrical network persists between growth stages.
