# MVP

## Purpose

Prove that the fundamental loop is fun:

**building a growing electrical network on a tactile miniature board.**

## Environment

River Village.

Includes:
- external MV connection
- village road
- river
- open sunny field
- wooded hill
- houses
- café
- future workshop site
- future station site

## Four stages

### Stage 1 — First Connection
Available:
- LV line
- MV line
- transformer
- external grid

Goal:
Supply houses and café.

### Stage 2 — Sunny Expansion
Add:
- more homes
- workshop

Unlock:
- solar

Problem:
Midday production helps, but evening peak remains.

### Stage 3 — Evening Peak
Add:
- transport demand

Unlock:
- battery

Problem:
Feeder/transformer constrained during evening.

### Stage 4 — Working Village
Add:
- light industry
- station
- cloudy weather

Problem:
Different demand profiles and multi-day adequacy.

## MVP tools

- external grid
- LV line
- MV line
- transformer
- solar
- battery

## MVP success criteria

The prototype succeeds if players can understand:
- why a transformer is needed,
- why more generation does not always solve a network problem,
- why solar timing matters,
- why battery MW and MWh are different,
- why battery location can matter,
- why a growing town changes the grid problem.

## Non-goals

Do not build:
- full city simulation
- money market
- procedural maps
- AC power flow
- frequency
- reactive power
- coal/nuclear campaign
- multiplayer
- complex disasters
